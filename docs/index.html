Who We Are

Our Story

Three years ago, two veterans and longtime friends came together with a shared vision and strong work ethic to start a fence company. Built on discipline, loyalty, and pride in doing things the right way, the company quickly became something we were proud of.

"We didn't just build fences — we built something we believed in, the same way we served: with everything we had."

As life evolved, so did our paths. After a long wait to find the perfect home, my daughter transferred back to the Dallas area for college, and I made the decision to stay in DFW to be close to family and begin a new chapter. With that, we chose to respectfully dissolve the original company and go our separate ways.

From that transition, Lifeline Fence was born. A name that means something — a line you hold on to, the one that keeps everything else standing. That's what we build for you.
